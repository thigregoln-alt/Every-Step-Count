### Every Step Counts – A Week of Energy and Solidarity!

From **November 16 to 22**, ATEC Palmela joins the **#TodosOsPassosContam** initiative, promoted by the Galp Foundation, turning physical activity into meals for those in need.

Our mission is to contribute to the national goal of donating **1.5 million meals** to the Food Emergency Network (*Rede de Emergência Alimentar*).

## 🎯 Our Challenge

We are bringing our classes together in a friendly competition where every step counts!

- 📱 **StepUp:** Each class creates its own group to track daily steps.
- 📊 **Class Representatives:** Collect daily results, convert steps into kilometres and submit the data through this website.
- 🤝 **One Team:** All class results contribute to ATEC Palmela's overall progress.

## 🏆 Daily Awards

We celebrate the effort and dedication of our community through four daily distinctions:

- 🏆 **Class Champion:** Most kilometres accumulated.
- 🚀 **Breakout Class:** Biggest daily improvement.
- 👟 **Golden Stride:** Highest individual step count.
- 🤝 **Team Spirit:** 100% of registered class members active.

Follow the leaderboard, compare class performances and discover our daily achievements through this website.
